--- layout: ontology_details ontology_id: OGMS ontology_title: Ontology of General Medical Science short_description: An ontology for representing treatment of disease and diagnosis and on carcinomas and other pathological entities. description: The Ontology for General Medical Science (OGMS) is based on the papers Toward an Ontological Treatment of Disease and Diagnosis and On Carcinomas and Other Pathological Entities. The ontology attempts to address some of the issues raised at the Workshop on Ontology of Diseases (Dallas, TX) and the Signs, Symptoms, and Findings Workshop(Milan, Italy). OGMS was formerly called the clinical phenotype ontology. Terms from OGMS hang from the Basic Formal Ontology. See http://ontology.buffalo.edu/medo/Disease_and_Diagnosis.pdf homepage: github.com/OGMS/ogms homepage_url: https://github.com/OGMS/ogms ---
