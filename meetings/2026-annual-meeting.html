CTSOC 2026 Annual Meeting

When: Tuesday, September 15, 2026, from 9:30 am–5:30 pm EDT.

Who: The Clinical Translational Science Ontology Consortium (CTSOC) is a consortium of ontology developers and users from across the USA focused on using ontologies to promote the interoperability of biomedical data in clinical translational science.

What: The 2026 CTSOC Meeting is a two-part event with an in-person workshop session in the morning and a hybrid presentation session in the afternoon that will feature a series of talks on leveraging AI and ontologies in translational science. This is the second event in a series (details of the kickoff event are available here.)

Where: Hybrid via Zoom and at the UB's National AI Institute for Exceptional Education (AI4EE) Frontier Space Conference Room, Lockwood Memorial Library, Room 205.
